protection of joint logistics operations IVO SPODs and APODs on the
coast to the south and east, as well as the GLOCs connecting those sites
to the ground forces fighting decisive actions in the west. We must
prevent the enemy from interfering in any way with logistics operations

operation focused on major mobility corridors and recently opened GLOCs
and APODs.

can range south/east of the southern/eastern boundaries of regimental
area of operations. RLT-2 is prepared to conduct a RIP with coalition
forces and transition to follow-on offensive operations.
